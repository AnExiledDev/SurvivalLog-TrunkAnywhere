# Changelog

## 0.4.6

- The log file `BepInEx\trunk-anywhere.log` no longer grows without limit. Past 1 MB, the older part moves to `trunk-anywhere.log.1`.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20719.

## 0.4.5

- Now uses Exiled's Toolkit for its shared parts, so it needs Exiled's Toolkit 0.19.0 or newer.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20719.

## 0.4.4

- New `[Looting] TakeAllToTrunk` setting turns the Take All to Trunk button off. It is on by default, and a change takes effect at once.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20719.

## 0.4.3

- The trunk key no longer opens the trunk while you type in a search box or any other text box in another mod's window. This needs Exiled's Toolkit 0.12.0 or newer.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20151.

## 0.4.2

- Updated for Survival Log 1.1.19611, which changed how the game checks its own hotkeys.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.19611.

## 0.4.1

- After a game update, Mod Manager can tell you if this mod may need an update.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.19131.

## 0.4.0

- Mod Manager's Mods screen links to this changelog.
- Settings changed on the Mod Manager's Mods screen take effect at once.
- Mod Manager's Mods screen shows the mod's name, description, category and links.
- Exiled's Toolkit 0.4.0 or newer is optional. With it, settings you change on the Mods screen take effect right away. Without it, they take effect the next time you start the game.

## 0.3.0

- Fixed: the shop's trunk tab buys straight into the trunk again, the same as without the mod. Those items go on the same bill as your bag.
- You still can't drag an unpaid item into the trunk.

## 0.2.1

- First public release.
- Before the disaster, press F6 to open your vehicle's trunk from anywhere.
- You can't put unpaid shop items in the trunk.
