# Changelog

## 0.3.0

The shop's trunk tab buys straight into the trunk again, as it does without the mod. Those items go on the same bill as your bag. Dragging an unpaid item into the trunk is still refused.

## 0.2.1

First public release. Press F6 to open your vehicle's trunk from anywhere before the disaster. Unpaid shop items are kept out of the trunk.
